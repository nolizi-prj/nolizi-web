One product exists today. That number is the honest one, and it is more useful
than a roadmap.

Each product below is Apache-2.0, runs on Node 22 or in a container, needs no
account with anyone, and states its current limitation before it states its
features. Self-hosting is first-class permanently: nothing in the code knows
about a particular host.

What comes next is decided in the open —
[the backlog is public](https://github.com/pumasi-ai/pumasi-product-hunt),
ranked by a scored rubric, and the reasoning stays on the record.
