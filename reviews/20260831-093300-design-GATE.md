## What the objections cost, and bought

Claude's family objected twice. Both objections were cited, both were correct,
and neither was argued past.

**Round 1 — two accessibility defects, both confirmed by recomputation.**

1. `.terminal` inverted its ground to `--ink-strong` in light while `.ok`
   (3.67:1), `.warn` (3.65:1) and the `$` prompt (Sign, **2.88:1**) kept values
   that had only ever been measured against paper — under a floor
   `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §4 calls non-negotiable, one of them under 3:1.
   *Fixed:* `.terminal` restates the whole dark ramp and is a scoped scheme
   rather than a background swap. Worst pair is now 9.21:1.
2. `--line-strong` (1.92:1) was the sole visible boundary of `.input` and
   `.slot`, against the same section's stated 3:1 non-text minimum.
   *Fixed:* new `--line-control` at 3.35:1 light / 3.71:1 dark, for controls
   only; `--line-strong` keeps the card role.

It also found, outside the citation: `--signal` had no shipped consumer, and
the products page put a seeded mosaic — the loudest object on each card,
carrying no information, near-identical across all three — above the product
name and its stage badge. Both addressed: product cards now carry
`data-product` and their own published mark.

**Round 2 — the fix's own guard was weaker than claimed.** `test/contrast.test.ts`
read the terminal's ground from an assumed token name and seeded the terminal's
token map from the light scheme, so it caught a colour *dropped from* the ramp
and stayed green when the ground was moved *out from under* an untouched one —
which is the shape that actually shipped. The claim that it had been verified
to fail on reintroduction was true only of the narrower shape.
*Fixed:* the ground is parsed from the `background` declaration, the terminal
map holds only what `.terminal` itself declares, and resolution falls through
to the page exactly as a browser does. Both defect shapes now fail:

- ground moved, ramp untouched → 2 failures, 1.16:1 light and 1.27:1 dark
- one token dropped from the ramp → 1 failure, 3.70:1

**Round 3 — approved**, after the reviewer reproduced both experiments in a
scratch copy and probed two further vacuous-pass paths of its own. It raised one
non-blocking documentation error — §4 published the dark signal band as
"9.0–10.6" above a table reading 8.99 — which has also been fixed.
